About us

Te Puia is a 70-hectare geothermal park and Māori cultural center located in the historic Te Whakarewarewa Thermal Valley in Rotorua, New Zealand. It features the world-famous Pōhutu Geyser—the largest active geyser in the Southern Hemisphere erupting up to 30 meters high—bubbling mud pools, and traditional arts programs.
